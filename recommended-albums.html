The Genre Context

Dark country draws from a long American tradition. Understanding the roots deepens your appreciation. Seek out the old weird America — the Carter Family, Dock Boggs, early Hank Williams. Then the outlaw era — Waylon Jennings, Merle Haggard, Guy Clark. Then the gothic turn — the sounds that Dark Country Boy builds on today.
