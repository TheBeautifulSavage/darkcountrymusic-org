About This Song

"Rusted Dogtags, Rotten Roots" draws from the same dark well as Cash and Waylon and all the honest dead, On The Swamp Revenant, the song anchors an album-length study in what it costs to live honestly in hard country. It belongs in any playlist alongside the best that dark Americana has ever offered.
