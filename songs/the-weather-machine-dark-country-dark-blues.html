About This Song

"The Weather Machine" honors the tradition of outlaw balladry with muscle and grace, Included on Chemtrails & Cover-Ups, where hardship and beauty trade verses like old friends in a roadhouse booth. The kind of song that makes you feel less alone in the darkest kind of way.
